How do I get to the event?

Located at 152–166 Shore St W, Cleveland, Raby Bay Harbour sits right next to key transit hubs making it easy to get to.

Parking: Additional car parking available at 122 Middle Street, with short walk across the street.

Train:

  • Cleveland Line and get off at Cleveland train station which is 250m (3-4 min walk) via Shore Street West.

Bus:

  • Middle Street Bus Station (Stops A & B) is a 400m (5-6 minute walk) via Shore Street West.

Bus routes:

  • Birkdale - Route 255 (Birkdale to Cleveland via Wellington Point)

  • Capalaba - Route 250 (Carindale-Redland Bay via Capalaba & Cleveland) or Route 252 (Capalaba-Cleveland loop via Alexandra Hills)

  • Alexandra Hills - Route 250 or 252

  • Thornlands - Route 274 or 275 (via Panorama Dr / Finucane Rd)

  • Victoria Point - Route 250 (Main trunk route along Cleveland-Redland Bay Rd)

  • Redland Bay - Route 250 (Starts at Redland Bay Marina to Cleveland Station)
