Share your ideas, to help us shape the future of Raby Bay Harbour for all to enjoy the waterfront.

Fourfold Studio is working with Raby Bay Harbour, in collaboration with Redland City Council, to deliver a community consultation program and the activation event Bayside Backyard at the precinct to gather community sentiments of what the future of Raby Bay Harbour might hold.

How do I get to the event?

Bayside Backyard will be happening at 152–166 Shore St W, Cleveland, Raby Bay Harbour and is right next to key transit hubs making it easy to get to.

Parking: Additional car parking available on the corner of Wynyard St and Shore St W, with short walk across the street.

Train:

  • Cleveland Line and get off at Cleveland train station which is 250m (3-4 min walk) via Shore Street West.

Bus:

  • Middle Street Bus Station (Stops A & B) is a 400m (5-6 minute walk) via Shore Street West.

Bus routes:

  • Birkdale - Route 255 (Birkdale to Cleveland via Wellington Point)

  • Capalaba - Route 250 (Carindale-Redland Bay via Capalaba & Cleveland) or Route 252 (Capalaba-Cleveland loop via Alexandra Hills)

  • Alexandra Hills - Route 250 or 252

  • Thornlands - Route 274 or 275 (via Panorama Dr / Finucane Rd)

  • Victoria Point - Route 250 (Main trunk route along Cleveland-Redland Bay Rd)

  • Redland Bay - Route 250 (Starts at Redland Bay Marina to Cleveland Station)
